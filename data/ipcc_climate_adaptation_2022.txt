IPCC Climate Adaptation Report (2022)

Focus: Water availability and Species Survival

Analysis:
Water scarcity due to low rainfall in semi-arid and arid regions is a primary driver of biodiversity loss. Reduced precipitation leads to drying of natural habitats, threatening native species.

Interventions:
- Water Catchment and Agroforestry: Implementing water catchment systems alongside agroforestry reduces surface runoff by up to 30%. This improves soil moisture and raises groundwater levels.
- Species Survival: Areas implementing these combined land-use and water-retention practices observed a 40% higher survival rate of native bird and amphibian species during prolonged drought periods.
- Deforestation Impacts: Deforestation for agricultural expansion in these sensitive zones exacerbates temperature fluctuations and decreases local rainfall by up to 10%, accelerating habitat degradation.
